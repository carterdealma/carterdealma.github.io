About

The Pegasus Project is an investigation into the surveillance spyware Pegasus, developed by the Israeli company NSO Group. The spyware mainly spread via zero-click exploits on mobile devices.


The investigation, which launched in July 2021, revealed how governments around the world have used Pegasus to target journalists, activists, and political dissidents. It revealed widespread human rights violations and abuses of power.


This creative project serves to visualize which known countries have been implicated in the Pegasus Project, the scale of surveillance in each country, and the results of that surveillance.
