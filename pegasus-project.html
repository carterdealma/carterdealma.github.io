About

The Pegasus Project was an investigation led by 17 media organizations into the surveillance spyware Pegasus, developed by the Israeli company NSO Group. The spyware mainly spread via zero-click exploits on mobile devices.


The investigation, which launched in July 2021, revealed how governments around the world have used Pegasus to target journalists, activists, political dissidents, and more. It revealed widespread human rights violations and abuses of power.


This creative project serves to visualize which countries have been implicated in the Pegasus Project, the scale of surveillance in each country, and the results of that surveillance.
